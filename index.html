03 — HOW I WORK

Clear process.
No mystery.

A good result starts with understanding the problem—not jumping straight into code.

01

Understand

Clarify the goal, inputs, constraints, and what a successful result looks like.

02

Scope

Agree on deliverables, timeline, assumptions, and what is outside the project.

03

Build & test

Implement in small steps, test expected behavior, and handle common failure cases.

04

Hand over

Provide the files, setup notes, and a straightforward explanation of how to use the result.

✳

My working principle: be clear about what's done, what isn't, and what needs testing. No inflated promises.
